Welcome

Welcome to our Food Waste Management System. This website creates awareness about food waste and provides simple ways to reduce it.

About Food Waste

Food waste happens when food that is still safe to eat is thrown away or not used properly. It affects the environment and wastes valuable resources.

By managing food properly, we can save money, reduce waste and help people in need.

Ways to Reduce Food Waste

1. Plan Before Buying

Prepare a shopping list and buy only the food that you actually need.

2. Store Food Properly

Store food correctly to keep it fresh for a longer time.

3. Donate Extra Food

Safe and unused food can be donated to people who need it.

4. Make Compost

Suitable food waste can be converted into compost and used for plants.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create awareness about food waste and encourage people to use food responsibly.

Every small action can help us build a cleaner and healthier environment.
